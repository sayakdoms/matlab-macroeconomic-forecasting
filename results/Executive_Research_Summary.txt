MATLAB MACROECONOMETRICS PROJECT
==============================================

DATA
266 quarterly U.S. macroeconomic observations from 1960 to 2026.

BASELINE MODEL
The contemporaneous OLS model achieved an R-squared of 0.0198 and an adjusted R-squared of 0.0085.

DYNAMIC MODEL
Introducing lagged macroeconomic relationships increased adjusted R-squared to 0.6483, with an RMSE of 2.3984.

OUT-OF-SAMPLE FORECASTING
During the stable 2016-2019 pre-COVID period, the econometric model improved RMSE by 5.58 percent and MAE by 14.81 percent relative to naive persistence.

STRUCTURAL BREAK
A structural-break test around 2020 produced an F-statistic of 12.4344 with p-value 0.000000, indicating substantial parameter instability around the pandemic shock.

FINAL CONCLUSION
Dynamic macroeconomic relationships substantially improve historical explanatory fit, but strong structural instability limits forecast generalization during major regime changes. Adaptive expanding-window estimation improves some forecast characteristics but does not consistently outperform naive persistence over the full post-2016 test period.
